## 🧃 About the Project

**B.Laban** is a growing business operating across **Egypt, UAE, and KSA**. As the brand scales, understanding the story behind the numbers becomes just as important as the product itself.

This project turns B.Laban's raw operational data into **clear, actionable business insights** — covering performance, revenue, customer behavior, demand, and operational efficiency.

Think of it as swapping the blender for a dashboard. 🥤➡️📊

## 🧹 From Raw Data to Business Insights

Our workflow followed four key stages:

```
1. DATA CLEANING        →  Preparing reliable data
2. EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS →  Exploring patterns
3. BUSINESS INSIGHTS    →  Understanding findings
4. RECOMMENDATIONS      →  Supporting decisions
```

### 🧼 Data Cleaning Process
Messy data doesn't get a seat at the table. Here's how we tidied things up:

| Step | Action | Details |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Handle Outliers** | Fixed extreme values (e.g. `Unit_Price`) |
| 02 | **Handle Missing Values** | Addressed gaps in `Customer_Age`, `Store_Rating` |
| 03 | **Transform Data Types** | Corrected column types for accurate analysis |
| 04 | **Add New Effective Columns** | Engineered new variables to unlock deeper insights |

## 💡 Highlights from the Analysis

**🛒 Sales Performance**
- Identified top-performing product **categories** driving the most revenue
- Compared performance across **Egypt, UAE & KSA**
- Evaluated **in-store vs. online** order sources
- Assessed how **membership tiers** impact total revenue

**⭐ Customer Experience**
- Analyzed delivery speed & fulfillment times to optimize dispatch during peak demand
- Tracked average customer ratings across branches
- Measured how delivery/prep time impacts satisfaction
- Assessed loyalty & retention via membership participation

**🍹 Demand & Operations**
- **Peak hours** drive the highest order volumes — staffing & inventory should follow demand curves
- **Temperature** influences category preference and order quantity
- External conditions (weather, time of day) are key **demand signals**
- Insights support smarter **inventory and staffing decisions**
